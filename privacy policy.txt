Introduction


Welcome to the Word Counter app (“we,” “our,” or “us”).


Your privacy is very important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our mobile application. We want you to feel confident that your information is safe and secure, and we are committed to complying with applicable data protection and privacy laws.


This Privacy Policy applies only to the Word Counter app and explains our practices in detail. Because we understand that your text and written content may feel personal, we want to assure you that this app is designed to work without collecting, transmitting, or storing personal information on external servers. Any text you enter—such as words, sentences, or notes—is processed entirely on your device and is never sent to us.


By using our app, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.



---


1. About the Word Counter App


The Word Counter app is a simple productivity tool that helps users count words, characters, sentences, and text length. It is intended for writing, editing, educational use, and general text analysis.


The app is designed for convenience and efficiency. It does not replace professional writing tools, grammar checking software, or advanced editing systems.



---


2. Information We Collect


Our Word Counter app is designed with privacy in mind. The app does not require registration, accounts, or personal identification. However, we provide transparency regarding information that may be used or generated during app usage.


2.1 Information Entered by the User


You may manually enter or paste text into the app. This may include:


Words


Sentences


Paragraphs


Notes or personal writing


Any text you choose to analyze



This information is used solely to perform local text analysis. None of this data leaves your device.


2.2 Non-Personal Information


We may collect limited, non-identifiable information through the Google Play Store for analytics and crash reporting. This may include:


Device model and operating system


General app performance data


Error logs (in case of crashes)



This type of information does not identify you personally and is used to improve app stability.


2.3 No Sensitive Data Collection


We do not collect or request sensitive information such as:


Contact information


Financial data


Location


Personal identification


Any sensitive text content




---


3. How We Use the Information


Because the app does not transmit personal data to servers, the primary use of your input is local text analysis. Here’s how your information is used:


Counting words and characters


Displaying results within the app


Providing text statistics for educational or productivity use



In summary: the app uses your data only for local calculations. No data is stored online, shared with third parties, or sold.



---


4. Data Storage & Retention


Inputs you provide are stored only temporarily in the device’s memory while the app is open.


Once you close the app or clear the text fields, your data is erased.


We do not permanently store any text in databases or servers.


We do not back up or synchronize your data across devices.



Your content remains fully under your control at all times.

5. Third-Party Services


The Word Counter app is intentionally minimal and does not include intrusive third-party integrations. However, the following may be used:


Google Play Services (for distribution and updates)


Crash reporting tools (to improve app reliability)



These services may collect anonymous diagnostic data such as error logs or device information. We do not control how these services handle information.

6. Advertising & Monetization


In its default version, this app does not display third-party ads or collect advertising identifiers. If future versions introduce optional ads, this Privacy Policy will be updated and you will be notified.


We do not sell or rent your information to advertisers.

7. Children’s Privacy


The Word Counter app is suitable for all ages. However:


We encourage parents to supervise children when entering text.


The app does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.


If you are a parent and wish to remove any text entered by your child, simply uninstall the app—no data remains on external servers.

We take privacy and security seriously:
